# Data dictionary

Every column of every downloadable file, with its unit and provenance.

All files are generated by `build_data.py`, the build script of the companion
repository (https://github.com/rafael-f-menezes/li-s-polysulfide-benchmark),
from the authors' analysis exports. Nothing is hand-transcribed. The upstream
exports use bare column names; the unit suffix is added on export and is the
authoritative statement of units.

**Unit suffixes**

| suffix | unit |
|---|---|
| `_Ha` | Hartree (atomic units of energy) |
| `_eV` | electronvolt |
| `_kcal_mol` | kcal/mol |
| `_Ang` | ångström |
| `_au` | atomic units (electron density and its derivatives) |
| `_e` | elementary charge |
| `_cm1` | wavenumber, cm⁻¹ |
| `_km_mol` | km/mol (IR intensity) |
| `_A4_amu` | Å⁴/amu (Raman activity) |

Columns with no suffix are identifiers, integer counts, formal charges, or
genuinely dimensionless quantities. They are listed as such below (unit
`n/a` for identifiers and labels).

Level of theory: geometries, harmonic frequencies and thermal corrections are
M06-2X/def2-TZVPPD/SMD. Columns named `CCSD_*` or `*_ccsd_*` are composite-CCSD
values, `G = E_elec(CCSD) + [G − E_elec](M06-2X)`, with the CCSD single point
run in the same SMD continuum as the M06-2X calculation. `HOMO`, every
solvation free energy (`G_solvent − G_GP`, the gas phase having no CCSD) and
the NBO energies are M06-2X by construction. Every ΔG in these files is evaluated at a single level.

The neutral S₈ reference of every formation energy is the D<sub>4d</sub> crown
(the C<sub>s</sub> exo-endo ring is a higher local minimum, 0.42 eV above the
crown at CCSD).

## `energies/data_all.csv`

Raw per-species thermochemistry, 248 rows, three media (GP = gas phase).

| column | unit | meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `SOLVENT` | n/a | `GP`, `DMSO`, or `TEGDME` |
| `MOL` | n/a | species label, e.g. `Li2S6`, `Li2S6-1`, `S8-2` |
| `STOICH` | n/a | stoichiometry string as written in the Gaussian archive |
| `CHARGE` | n/a | formal charge, integer |
| `MULTI` | n/a | spin multiplicity, integer |
| `SCF_Ha` | Hartree | M06-2X SCF electronic energy |
| `E0+ZPVE_Ha` | Hartree | electronic energy + zero-point vibrational energy |
| `Eint_Ha` | Hartree | internal energy at 298.15 K |
| `E0+ENTHALPY_Ha` | Hartree | enthalpy at 298.15 K |
| `E0+GIBBS_Ha` | Hartree | Gibbs free energy at 298.15 K |
| `HOMO_eV` | eV | HOMO eigenvalue (M06-2X) |
| `CCSD_SCF_Ha` | Hartree | CCSD single-point electronic energy; blank where no CCSD run exists (all of GP) |
| `CCSD_GIBB_Ha` | Hartree | composite-CCSD Gibbs free energy |

Every energy in this file is in Hartree except `HOMO_eV`, which is in eV.

Of the 248 rows, 202 enter the analysis and 132 carry a composite-CCSD Gibbs
energy. The 46 rows set aside are the 41 exploratory x ≥ 9 rows (among them
`Li2S9-2`, an exact copy of `Li2S9-1` under the dianion's name), the duplicate
row `Li2S8-1 (copy)`, and the four electrolyte rows computed outside their own
medium (DMSO, TEGDME and TFSI⁻ in the gas phase, TEGDME in DMSO).

## `energies/polysulfide_thermodynamics_summary.csv`

Derived per-species formation energies, polysulfides only (electrolyte species
are excluded because the Li/S formation reference does not apply to them).

| column | unit | meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `SOLVENT`, `MOL` | n/a | as above |
| `family` | n/a | one of `Li2Sx`, `Li2Sx-1`, `Li2Sx-2`, `LiSx`, `LiSx-`, `Sx`, `Sx-1`, `Sx-2` |
| `n_Li`, `n_S` | n/a | atom counts, integer |
| `q` | n/a | formal charge, integer |
| `MULTI` | n/a | spin multiplicity, integer |
| `E0+GIBBS_Ha` | Hartree | M06-2X Gibbs free energy |
| `HOMO_eV` | eV | HOMO eigenvalue (M06-2X) |
| `dG_form_eV` | eV | formation Gibbs energy, M06-2X |
| `dG_form_per_atom_eV` | eV/atom | the same, per atom |
| `dG_form_per_S_eV` | eV/S | the same, per sulfur |
| `CCSD_GIBB_Ha` | Hartree | composite-CCSD Gibbs free energy |
| `dG_form_ccsd_eV` | eV | formation Gibbs energy, composite CCSD |
| `dG_form_ccsd_per_atom_eV` | eV/atom | the same, per atom |
| `dG_form_ccsd_per_S_eV` | eV/S | the same, per sulfur |
| `dG_ccsd_minus_dft_eV` | eV | CCSD minus M06-2X formation energy. Negate for the deviation as reported in the paper, which is defined M06-2X − CCSD |
| `set` | n/a | `main`, `SI`, or `ref` (species set of the paper; `ref` are the Li/Li⁺ reference rows, excluded from benchmark statistics) |

Formation energies follow ΔG = G − y·G(Li) − (x/8)·G(S₈) − n_e·μ_e with
n_e = −q and μ_e = G(Li) − G(Li⁺), all in-solvent and at a single level.

| `lap_au` | a.u. | Laplacian ∇²ρ at the BCP |

| `ELF`, `LOL` | dimensionless | electron localization function, localized orbital locator |
| `Ellipticity` | dimensionless | bond ellipticity |

## `nbo/nbo_*.csv`

Second-order perturbation donor–acceptor analysis (NBO 3.1, as implemented in
Gaussian 16) of the M06-2X/def2-TZVPPD/SMD wavefunctions at the optimized
geometries, both solvents; the analysis requires a self-consistent-field Fock
matrix, so it is not taken from the CCSD densities of the QTAIM analysis.
`nbo_all_full.csv` is the complete set (601 rows: 591 polysulfide interactions
over 69 species plus the TFSI⁻ reference); the split files select one channel.
Each molecule contributes every interaction of at least half its largest E(2)
(the selection rule of the NBO analysis, Supplementary Note 11 of the paper).
The S₈ rows are those of the D<sub>4d</sub> crown in both solvents.

| column | unit | meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `SOLVENT`, `MOL` | n/a | medium and species |
| `Donor Bonding`, `Acceptor Bonding` | n/a | NBO type, e.g. `LP`, `BD`, `LP*` |
| `Donor Species`, `Acceptor Species` | n/a | atoms involved |
| `E2_kcal_mol` | kcal/mol | second-order stabilization energy E(2) |

## `vibrational/`

Harmonic M06-2X frequencies.

Two scaling conventions are used, and each file states which one it carries.
The study uses 0.96 for the solvent and TFSI⁻ modes, the factor fitted to 19
measured neat-solvent infrared bands (least-squares optimum 0.963, mean
absolute deviation 7 cm⁻¹), and reports the polysulfide bands unscaled. The
two S–S calibration points that bracket the polysulfide window return different
factors: the strongest Raman mode of the S₈ crown implies 0.94 and the S₃•⁻
stretch measured by resonance Raman in the prepared solutions implies 0.959,
neither being the terminal S–S stretch of a closed-shell chain, while it is the
unscaled closed-shell prediction that matches the measured infrared solution
band and follows its composition trend; the unscaled positions therefore carry
a measured systematic of 20 to 30 cm⁻¹. `dominant_summary.csv`,
`fingerprint_*.csv`, `reference_band_peaks.csv` and `diagnostic_peaks_*.csv`
carry 0.96-scaled values for every species; `modes_all.csv` and the tables of
the interactive site carry the unscaled harmonics, with the paper's convention
as a second column or in parentheses. Divide or multiply by 0.96 to convert.
The difference is about 4 %, roughly 20 cm⁻¹ in the fingerprint window, which
is larger than the composition-driven shift the measurements resolve, so the
two conventions should not be mixed.

Scaling applies to reported band positions only. The zero-point, enthalpy and
entropy corrections behind every energy in `energies/` use the unscaled
harmonic frequencies, and no energy in this dataset depends on the convention.

- `modes_all.csv`: every harmonic normal mode of every species, 977 rows: the
  37 solution species, the two solvent molecules and TFSI⁻ (`family`
  `Solvent` / `Anion-ref`), in both solvents. `solvent`, `species`, `family`,
  `n_Li`, `n_S`, `q` identify the calculation; `mode_index` is the Gaussian
  mode number and `mode_label` its symmetry label; `freq_cm1` is the unscaled
  harmonic frequency; `freq_paper_cm1` the same under the paper's convention
  (×0.96 for `Solvent` and `Anion-ref` rows, unscaled otherwise);
  `ir_act_km_mol` the IR intensity and `raman_act_A4_amu` the Raman activity.
  The interactive spectrum viewer of the companion site is drawn from this
  table.
- `dominant_summary.csv`: strongest band per species: `raman_DMSO_cm1`,
  `raman_TEGDME_cm1`, `ir_DMSO_cm1`, `ir_TEGDME_cm1`, all cm⁻¹, ×0.96.
- `fingerprint_raman_*.csv`: top three Raman bands: `f1_cm1`…`f3_cm1` in cm⁻¹,
  activities `a1_A4_amu`…`a3_A4_amu` in Å⁴/amu.
- `fingerprint_ir_*.csv`: top three IR bands: `f1_cm1`…`f3_cm1` in cm⁻¹,
  intensities `a1_km_mol`…`a3_km_mol` in km/mol.
- `reference_band_peaks.csv`: electrolyte and solvent reference bands:
  `ir_freq_cm1` (cm⁻¹), `ir_act_km_mol` (km/mol), `raman_freq_cm1` (cm⁻¹),
  `raman_act_A4_amu` (Å⁴/amu); `rank` is an integer.
- `diagnostic_peaks_*.csv`: bands unique to a species against the reference
  set: `raman_unique_cm1` and `ir_unique_cm1` are cm⁻¹ lists,
  `n_raman_unique` / `n_ir_unique` are counts, `identifiable` is boolean.
- `chain_length_snapshot_weights.csv`: `w_chain_length_snapshot` is a
  dimensionless weight.
- `functional_ladder_check.csv`, `functional_ladder_verdicts.csv`: the
  cross-functional check of the Li₂S₇/Li₂S₈ strongest-IR-band ordering
  (Supplementary Note 1.1 of the paper): windowed band positions in cm⁻¹ per
  functional/basis and the verdict per case.
- `raman_solution_bands.csv`: measured Raman of the prepared polysulfide
  solutions in TEGDME (638 nm), one row per spectrum: `series` (`no_salt` or
  `LiFSI`), `nominal_x` (preparation label, not a species; the paper prints it
  as the Li:S preparation ratio 1:(x−1)), the S₃•⁻ progression
  on the recorded axis (`*_raw_cm1`) and after the per-spectrum axis correction
  (`*_cm1`), `delta_cm1` (axis offset), `omega_cm1` and `omega_x_cm1` (harmonic
  wavenumber and anharmonicity of the progression), `*_fwhm_cm1` and `*_err_cm1`
  in cm⁻¹, `*_rel` and `s8_*_rel` dimensionless amplitudes relative to ν₁, and
  `method` (`overtones` for all seven spectra; `combination` would mark a
  spectrum whose range stopped before the first overtone). Both series are
  baseline-corrected, smoothed and normalized to the integrated intensity of the
  TEGDME band at 1400–1500 cm⁻¹, so the `*_amp` columns compare between spectra
  relative to the solvent.
- `s3_anchor.csv`: the S₃•⁻ scale-factor anchor: `harmonic_cm1`, `measured_cm1`,
  `predicted_cm1`, `deviation_cm1` in cm⁻¹, `scale` and `lambda_implied`
  dimensionless, per solvent, mode and candidate factor.

## `si_extra_figures/`

Fifteen PDF figures that the Supplementary Information deposits with the data;
it prints the Li₂S₈ member of each series. `si_freqbench_Li2S2.pdf` …
`si_freqbench_Li2S8.pdf` show the gas-phase harmonic frequencies of each Li₂Sₓ
across six functional/basis-set combinations, and `si_freqheat_Li2S.pdf` …
`si_freqheat_Li2S8.pdf` the atomic participation ratio heatmaps of the normal
modes in TEGDME. Their source tables are not part of this dataset.

## Flagged entries

Three entries are retained with a flag (the caveats section of the paper's
Methods and Supplementary Note 12). The data files carry no flag column: the
entries are listed here and marked on the interactive site.

1. Neutral LiS₃• in DMSO is not a minimum. The SMD optimization terminated
   normally on a C<sub>2v</sub> first-order saddle point with one imaginary mode
   at 60i cm⁻¹ (IR 57 km/mol, force constant 0.026 mDyn/Å) and no minimum was
   located. Its energies are present at both levels; the thermal corrections
   omit the imaginary mode, so its Gibbs energy is an upper bound, high by about
   0.03 eV from that mode alone. Its QTAIM rows and geometry (saddle-point CCSD
   density) are included. The TEGDME entry is a minimum but sits 0.43 eV above
   its own gas-phase free energy, plausibly a higher isomer, so the two are not
   solvent-comparable.
2. Li₂S₃²⁻ in DMSO is flagged suspect: its raw free energy lies above that of
   Li₂S₃⁻, inverting the TEGDME ordering. It is excluded from the redox-ladder
   figure and statistics.
3. LiS₅⁻ in TEGDME has monodentate lithium coordination where every comparable
   species is bidentate, and a formation energy 0.9 eV above its own ladder;
   this is possibly a genuine coordination difference, so its energies are
   retained. (Its gas-phase entry, once a solvated run misfiled as gas phase,
   was recomputed in vacuum and carries no flag.)

The three non-nuclear attractors (Li₂S²⁻ in both solvents, Li₂S₃²⁻ in DMSO)
are not flags: with the NNA basin counted every system conserves charge, and
they are reported as a finding on the diffuse dianion density.

Species with x ≥ 9 exist in exploratory runs and are excluded from all analyses.

A spin-state audit of all rows and all CCSD input decks found three single
points that had been run as closed-shell singlets against triplet ground states
(the S atom in DMSO, and S₂ in both solvents). All three were recomputed as
triplets and the corrected values are what these files contain.
